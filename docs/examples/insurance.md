## Cost-sensitive threshold check

Investigating instead of standard handling costs 870 on an honest claim and saves 9,232 on an average fraudulent one. The Bayes threshold is FP / (FP + FN) = **0.086**; the empirical cost-minimising cut on the calibrated scores is **0.171** (precision 36%, recall 54%). The grid search over the full model picked 0.1, which also accounts for amount-dependent leakage and the table's hard routing rules.

    kind: intermediate
    table:
      name: triage
      hit_policy: FIRST
      inputs: [fraud_p, claim_type, injury, amount]
      outputs: [lane]
      rules:
        - {when: [">= investigate_at", "-", "-", "-"], then: ['"investigate"'],
           annotation: fraud likelihood at or above the investigation threshold}
        - {when: ["-", '"liability"', "-", "> 20000"], then: ['"standard"'],
           annotation: large liability claims always get an adjuster}
        - {when: ["-", "-", "true", "-"], then: ['"standard"'],
           annotation: bodily injury needs manual handling}
        - {when: ["< 0.1", '"auto", "property"', "-", "<= fast_track_max"], then: ['"fast-track"'],
           annotation: low-risk small auto or property claim}
