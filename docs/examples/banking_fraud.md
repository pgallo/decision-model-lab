# Banking fraud: alert disposition under investigator capacity

Synthetic data, seed 23. Generated by `decision-lab run banking_fraud`.

- calibrated score: Brier 0.0020, ECE 0.0009

100,000 synthetic transactions over 20 days (fraud rate 0.69%). Days 0 to 9 train a gradient boosting classifier, days 10 to 13 fit Platt scaling, the last 6 days are held out for every comparison below.

## Threshold optimisation

| Setting | Threshold | Alerts/day (test) | Precision (test) | Recall (test) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bayes threshold from the cost matrix | 0.015 |  |  |  |
| cost-optimal, no capacity limit | 0.025 | 99.5 | 0.291 | 0.972 |
| cost-optimal, at most 40 alerts/day | 0.248 | 34.0 | 0.701 | 0.799 |

Thresholds are fitted on the calibration days. With a 40/day team the binding constraint is capacity, not cost: the unconstrained optimum would generate 100 alerts/day, so the optimiser raises the threshold until the queue fits.

## Strategy comparison on held-out days

| Strategy | Alerts/day | Peak day | Precision | Value recall | Loss prevented/day | Net of investigation cost/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rules only | 8.167 | 10.0 | 0.796 | 0.468 | 25,123.5 | 24,919.3 |
| model only (probability threshold) | 34.0 | 40.0 | 0.701 | 0.897 | 48,177.7 | 47,327.7 |
| model + rules (hard rules, then expected value) | 40.0 | 40.0 | 0.600 | 0.949 | 50,999.3 | 49,999.3 |

Capacity is 40 alerts/day. Model strategies keep the top of each day's queue up to capacity (alerts beyond it are never worked). The rules-only strategy has no capacity control: its queue is whatever the rules produce. The combined strategy lowers the score cut to a third and ranks by probability x amount, so capacity goes to the alerts with the largest expected loss.

## Human-in-the-loop routing

Three lanes: auto-act (block the payment without review; wrongly blocking a legitimate payment costs 400 in friction and attrition), human review (costs 25, assumed to resolve the case correctly), reject (let it through). Review volume is capped at 40/day; thresholds are fitted on the calibration days and applied to the test days.

| Lane | Probability band | Transactions |
|---|---|---|
| auto-act | >= 0.350 | 180 |
| human-review | 0.050 to 0.350 | 238 |
| reject | < 0.050 | 29582 |

On the test days: 45 legitimate payments auto-blocked, 13 frauds let through.
